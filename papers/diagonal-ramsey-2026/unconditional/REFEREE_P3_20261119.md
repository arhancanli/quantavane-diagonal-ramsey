FINDINGS

GAP-1 (P3.1, proof of (R2)). The first term is "bounded by |d_1| plus the jumps passed"; the second "at most the jumps of d there. Summing, ... at most V_d." A knot u_j in (theta, (n0+1)/k) is charged twice (in the first term's "jumps passed" and again in the second term), so the stated bounds add to up to 2V_d. The bound V_d is nevertheless TRUE. Supply: for s in (theta, omega], d(s) = d_1 + sum_{j<M} (d_{j+1} - d_j) 1[s > u_j]; counting integers in (k u_j, t] (k u_j > k theta >= n0) against the length (t - k u_j)_+ gives
  Gamma(t) - kG(t/k) = d_1 {k theta} + sum_{j: k u_j < t} (d_{j+1} - d_j) {k u_j},  {x} = x - floor(x) in [0,1),
so |Gamma(t) - kG(t/k)| <= V_d. Checked exactly on 4,000 random instances (2,259 with a knot in (theta, (n0+1)/k)); worst ratio 0.975. Alternative: prove 2V_d and double V_d in the three K_J conditions.
